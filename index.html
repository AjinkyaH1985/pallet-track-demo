    About this demo
    • Every pallet has its own QR code. Scan it when it goes out (LOAD OUT) and when it comes back (UNLOAD IN).
    • Data stays on this device only, in this browser. Two phones (or a phone and a computer) do not share data. Clearing the browser deletes the data.
    • Saved scans cannot be changed or deleted. If you make a mistake, scan again.
    • Camera needs an https:// link. Allow camera access when asked.
